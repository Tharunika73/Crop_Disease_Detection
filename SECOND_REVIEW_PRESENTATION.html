17-07-2023 SECOND REVIEW PRESENTATION 5
MODULES
Architectural & Functional Breakdown
  • Module 1: Leaf Area Isolation & Severity Quantification
    Transforms images to HSV space, generates green-leaf binary masks, and applies Otsu's adaptive thresholding on inverted saturation channel to calculate the exact percentage of infected tissue.
  • Module 2: Deep Learning Disease Identification & Explainable AI (XAI)
    Lightweight MobileNetV2 classifier diagnoses plant pathology. Grad-CAM visual heatmaps overlay saliency maps directly onto the leaf image to verify symptoms for the user.
  • Module 3: Dynamic Multi-Factor Risk Assessment Engine
    Integrates infection severity, real-time weather (temp & humidity), phenological vulnerability, and prior scan trajectory into an objective 0–100 risk score with tiered danger bands.
  • Module 4: Actionable Advisory, Voice Playback & Conversational AI
    Provides tiered organic, cultural, and chemical mitigation protocols, text-to-speech audio in regional languages, and an AI chatbot grounded in real scan data.
  • Module 5: Regional Outbreak Surveillance & Geo-Epidemiological Map
    Aggregates scans by region, calculates local infection densities, and automatically triggers epidemic warnings to prevent wide-scale crop damage.
17-07-2023 SECOND REVIEW PRESENTATION 6
ADVANTAGES AND DISADVANTAGES
Critical Engineering Appraisal
Key Advantages
  • Proactive Risk Forecasting: Combines real-time microclimate and growth stage to forecast danger before catastrophic crop loss occurs.
  • Transparent XAI: Grad-CAM eliminates the "black-box" dilemma by visually showing farmers where lesions were detected.
  • Targeted Agro-Chemical Management: Tiered advisory avoids indiscriminate chemical spraying, preserving soil microbiome.
  • Community-Level Protection: Regional outbreak surveillance isolates infectious hotspots early.
  • High Accessibility: Web-based responsive design with multi-lingual voice playback for rural farmers.
Limitations & Mitigations
  • Dependence on Image Clarity: Out-of-focus or severely shadowed photos can degrade segmentation (mitigated via minimum pixel threshold fallback).
  • Internet Dependency for Weather: Live weather forecasting requires connectivity (mitigated via simulated local defaults and offline cache).
